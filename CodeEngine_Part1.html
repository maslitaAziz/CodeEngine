Part 1: Basic Elements, Selection and Repetition

Seven short topics and a coding challenge, about 75 minutes. Each topic starts with Learn first: a short explanation, a step-by-step walkthrough you can play, and a quick practice that is not marked. Then you do the activity, which is marked. Each activity opens after you submit the one before it, and your answers are recorded under your matric number.
